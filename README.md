<h1>Local Cafe and Bar Prices</h1>

<h2>Application that allows users to explore and compare happy hours and prices of drinks (beers, wine and coffee for the moment) at local cafes and bars in their area. The prices are maintained and updated by the local users so the information on the web app is as accurate as the active users. Feel free to join us! </h2>
